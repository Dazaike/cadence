Cadence is a private, open-source health dashboard for the data stored in Android's
Health Connect (and, optionally, Samsung Health). It only ever reads, and it has no ads
or trackers. By default nothing you see leaves your device; there is an optional
cloud sync you have to sign in to before anything is uploaded.

Features:

* Overview, a customizable widget grid with named layouts: daily activity rings, steps,
  heart, sleep, recovery, body, goals and a consistency heatmap.
* Activity, Heart, Sleep and Body pages with trends, goal lines, weekday patterns, heart-rate
  zones, sleep schedule and stages. Days without data are gaps, never zeros.
* Insights computed on your device: readiness estimate, week-over-week changes, sleep
  consistency and correlations, clearly labelled as estimates.
* Calendar with a month view and a day timeline.
* Light, dark or system theme, a selectable accent colour, and an Imperial or Metric choice.
* Home-screen widgets in the same neutral palette.
* Export, save your accessible data to a CSV or JSON file, or share Markdown with an AI assistant.
* Optional cloud sync, sign in to your own Cadence cloud account to see the same data
  on a web dashboard and let AI assistants read it through a remote MCP server. Off until
  you sign in; sign out any time.

Cadence reads data through Health Connect, so it works alongside whatever apps and
wearables already write there. You stay in control of exactly which data types it can
read, and can change or revoke access at any time from Health Connect.
